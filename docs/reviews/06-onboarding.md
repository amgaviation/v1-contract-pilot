# Onboarding & first-run review

### [CRITICAL] Signup's confirmation email is broken, so no real pilot can ever reach the wizard
- Where: `README.md:74-79`; funnel path `/signup` → `/check-email` → `/auth/confirm` → `/welcome` (payment) → `/onboarding`; `app/(auth)/signup/actions.ts:98,108,147` (confirmation is enabled on the Supabase project and the confirmed session lands at `/welcome`)
- Issue: README's own Status section states plainly: *"signup returns `Error sending confirmation email`. SMTP is configured against Resend, credentials are accepted, and the send is rejected with `550 — the sending domain is not verified`… no new pilot can complete signup"* until `amgaviationgroup.com` is verified at Resend or the sender is temporarily pointed at `onboarding@resend.dev`. Email confirmation gates login (`app/(auth)/signup/actions.ts:147`'s own comment), and login gates `/welcome`, which is where payment happens — so this is not a friction point inside onboarding, it is a wall in front of it. Every finding below assumes a pilot who reached `/onboarding`; today, none can, by any path, including the ones who already tried to pay.
- Fix: verify the sending domain at resend.com/domains (DKIM + SPF), or point the sender at `onboarding@resend.dev` as a temporary unblock, per README's own instructions. Nothing in the onboarding wizard or first-run app is reachable until this is fixed.

- Where: `app/(app)/overview/page.tsx:1134-1141` (step 1, "Add your first client"), `:1319` (getting-started subtitle), `:1197-1205` (page-header CTAs); `app/(app)/trips/trip-form.tsx:79-81` (`NO_CLIENT`); `app/(app)/invoices/new/draft-form.tsx:136-139,211` (clientless trips can't be billed); `app/(app)/trips/[id]/page.tsx:351-356` ("Invoice it")
- Issue: The Getting Started panel's own ordered list puts `"Add your first client"` first (`overview/page.tsx:1137`, done-state gates every later step), but its subtitle sentence directly beside it reads `` `${stepsDone} of ${GETTING_STARTED_STEPS.length} done. Start with a trip. The figures below fill in from there.` `` (`overview/page.tsx:1319`) — and the two persistent, most visually prominent buttons on this exact screen, `Log a trip` (outline) and the one accent-filled button on the page, `Create invoice` (`overview/page.tsx:1197-1205`), are both reachable with zero clients on file. `/trips/new`'s picker offers `"No client yet"` as a first-class, saveable choice (`trip-form.tsx:79-81`, comment: *"a real, postable choice (client_id is optional)"*). But a clientless trip cannot generate invoice lines: `draft-form.tsx:136-139`'s own comment states *"Trips belong to a client; none can be billed on a clientless invoice (invoice_lines_validate_trip refuses it)"*, and its trip-selection UI never renders without a client selected (`:211`). ### [HIGH] The one first-run help topic that explains what a pilot was just charged is wrong
- Where: `lib/help/guide.ts:62-68` (topic `creating-account`), specifically line 65; `lib/stripe/server.ts:59-78` (`INTRO_FIRST_MONTH_CENTS`/`INTRO_FIRST_MONTH_LABEL`); `app/(auth)/welcome/page.tsx` (no trial mentioned); route `/help`
- Issue: `/help`'s "Creating your account" topic — the first topic under "Getting started," the section a confused Day-1 pilot would open — says: *"Once you are signed in you land on the plan screen, where you choose a tier and a billing interval and start a trial, card required. The exact trial length and the price of each tier and interval are shown live there…"* (`lib/help/guide.ts:65`). There is no trial. `lib/stripe/server.ts`'s own comment on `INTRO_FIRST_MONTH_CENTS` states the $5-first-month coupon *"replaced the 7-day free trial"* (`lib/stripe/server.ts:59-64`), and the actual `/welcome` screen never uses the word "trial" anywhere in its copy. A pilot who just had a card charged $5 and opens Help to double-check what they signed up for is told a materially different, and more reversible-sounding, story than what actually happened.
- Fix: rewrite `lib/help/guide.ts:62-68` to describe the shipped model: pick a tier and interval, enter a card, a monthly plan's first month is `$5` (from `INTRO_FIRST_MONTH_LABEL`) with the regular price from month two, annual plans are billed the full annual amount immediately, no trial.

### [MEDIUM] The mandatory onboarding gate has no sign-out
- Where: `lib/supabase/account.ts:206-216` (`requireAccount`'s unconditional onboarding redirect — every request, read or write, until `onboarding_complete` is true); `app/(onboarding)/layout.tsx:21-25` (comment: *"there is deliberately nowhere to go from here except through the wizard or past it"*); no `signOut`/`/login` reference anywhere under `app/(onboarding)` (verified by search); route `/onboarding`
- Issue: Every page in the product bounces a not-yet-onboarded, paid account straight back to `/onboarding`, on every single request (`account.ts:214-216`), and the wizard's own route group renders no chrome beyond a static mark and the label "Account setup" (`app/(onboarding)/layout.tsx:31-39`). There is no sign-out control anywhere in this flow — confirmed by the layout's own comment that this is deliberate. A pilot on the wrong browser profile, a shared device, or who simply wants to stop and come back as a different account has no visible way out except finishing or clicking "Skip for now."
- Fix: add a small sign-out link to the onboarding header (mirroring the `signOut` form `/welcome` already renders in its `AuthFooter`), so the wizard has one way out that isn't "finish" or "skip."

### [MEDIUM] The wizard has no autosave or resume — a reload mid-fill loses everything typed
- Where: `app/(onboarding)/onboarding/onboarding-wizard.tsx:82` (`const [step, setStep] = useState(0)`, plain client state); `:56-70` (header comment on why `defaultValue` prefers the echoed submit over props — only a rejected *submit* survives, nothing else does); `app/(onboarding)/onboarding/page.tsx:27-49` (what actually persists: only what signup's metadata already wrote to the account row)
- Issue: Step position and every field's typed value live only in React state until the pilot clicks "Finish setup" or "Skip for now." A reload, a crashed tab, or navigating away mid-wizard (say, after filling Business and Certificate but before Rates) returns the pilot to step 0 on the next visit with nothing but whatever `legal_name`/`home_base`/`kind` signup already prefilled (`onboarding/page.tsx:27-49`) — every field typed inside the wizard itself is gone, silently.
- Fix: persist the current step index in the URL (`?step=`) at minimum so a reload doesn't silently reset to step 1; the wizard's short length and near-total optionality make a full field-level autosave lower priority than fixing the silent step reset.
