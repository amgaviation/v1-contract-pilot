# Strategy review — positioning, funnel, measurement

Scope: `app/(marketing)/*`, every CTA and destination, the signup path (`/signup` →
`/check-email` → `/welcome` → Stripe checkout → `/(onboarding)`), and confirmed absences
(lead capture, newsletter, demo, contact, blog/SEO surface, referral, social). Audited
against the `marketing-plan` skill's embedded 17-section current-state rubric as an
analytical lens. `docs/MARKETING.md`'s positioning is treated as a signed, deliberate
2026-08-17 decision — not relitigated here except where evidence shows the execution
disagreeing with it. Copy quality and CRO/UX mechanics are out of scope (other agents).

---

### [CRITICAL] Every marketing CTA funnels through one currently-broken step
- Where: `app/(marketing)/page.tsx` (hero + close CTAs → `/signup`), `app/(marketing)/pricing/page.tsx` (3 tier cards + close band → `/signup`), `app/(marketing)/site-header.tsx:69-74`, `app/(marketing)/site-footer.tsx` (Account column), `app/(auth)/signup/actions.ts:32-42` (`toCheckEmail`), `app/(auth)/check-email/page.tsx`; corroborated by `README.md:74-79` and `docs/LAUNCH-GATES.md:386-391,593-595`
- Issue: There is exactly one funnel in this product — every CTA on every marketing page terminates at `/signup`, and `/signup` cannot complete without a Supabase confirmation email (`app/(auth)/signup/actions.ts` → `/check-email`). That send is currently rejected: `README.md` states "signup returns `Error sending confirmation email`... rejected with `550 — the sending domain is not verified`... no new pilot can complete signup." `docs/LAUNCH-GATES.md` independently confirms the same fact under G5 and adds a sharper strategic framing in its own "Open product decisions" section: *"Email confirmation is ON at signup, so a card-required trial starts with a 'check your email' wall. A deliberate decision to revisit, not an oversight — and G5's sending-domain problem makes it currently fatal rather than merely frictional."* There is no alternate path to an account anywhere on the site. This is a single point of failure by construction, not an edge case: 100% of top-of-funnel intent, from any channel, currently dead-ends at the same wall.
- Fix: Not a copy or strategy fix — a DNS/ops fix already specified in `README.md:76-78`: verify `amgaviationgroup.com` at resend.com/domains (DKIM + SPF), or temporarily point the sender at `onboarding@resend.dev`. Strategic consequence to act on regardless: do not direct paid, content, or outreach effort at the funnel until this clears, and add a live signup-to-confirmation smoke test as a pre-condition for any future acquisition push.

### [HIGH] The site's only conversion ask is binary — card-required $5 intro — with no lower-commitment path, so a non-converting visitor leaves no trace anywhere
- Where: `app/(marketing)/page.tsx` (hero + close, 2 CTAs), `app/(marketing)/pricing/page.tsx` (3 tier cards + close band, 4 CTAs), `app/(marketing)/site-header.tsx`, `app/(marketing)/site-footer.tsx` — 8 primary CTAs across the whole marketing surface, all resolving to the same one action; verified absent: no email-capture field, waitlist, or newsletter signup anywhere under `app/(marketing)` or `app/(auth)`
- Issue: Every primary CTA on the site — the header, the footer, both landing CTAs, and all four pricing-page CTAs — offers exactly one action: create an account and enter a card for the $5 intro month. (This finding does not question the intro-price-over-trial decision itself, which `docs/MARKETING.md` §3 and `lib/stripe/server.ts` document as a deliberate, owner-signed replacement for a free trial — that call is not being relitigated.) What's missing is a *second*, lower-commitment conversion option for a visitor who reads the pricing and isn't ready to pay that day. Combined with the absence of any tracking pixel or analytics (next finding) and the absence of any email-capture mechanism, a visitor who does not convert on this visit is not merely unconverted — they are unrecorded and unreachable: no retargeting audience to build, no address to add to a nurture sequence, nothing. The funnel's only two possible outcomes are "paid" and "gone, with no record."
- Fix: Add one lower-commitment capture point distinct from the paid $5 signup — e.g., an email field on `/pricing` ("not ready yet — we'll follow up") — additive to, not a replacement for, the existing card-required offer. Smallest version would live in `app/(marketing)/pricing/page.tsx`'s closing band alongside the existing "Start for $5" CTA.

### [HIGH] Zero measurement instrumentation anywhere in the pre-payment funnel
- Where: absent — verified against `package.json:39-59` (11 runtime dependencies, no analytics/tracking package among them), `app/layout.tsx`, `app/(marketing)/layout.tsx` (no analytics script or tag in either), and a repository-wide search for GA4/gtag/PostHog/Segment/Mixpanel/Amplitude/Plausible/Fathom/Vercel Analytics/`dataLayer` (the only matches were incidental string collisions in verify scripts, migration files, and unrelated skill-reference docs — none are real instrumentation)
- Issue: Nothing observes the funnel between "stranger arrives" and "Stripe subscription exists." No pageview or event tracking on the four marketing pages, no CTA-click tracking, no UTM capture, and no stage-by-stage funnel tracking across `/signup` → `/check-email` → `/welcome` → checkout → onboarding. Stripe itself gives revenue-side visibility once a subscription exists, but everything upstream of checkout — which CTA a visitor used, where they dropped off, whether the $5 offer is actually the reason someone converted — is completely unmeasured. Once the blocker above is fixed, there will be no way to tell whether the fix (or any future acquisition effort) worked beyond watching raw signup counts.
- Fix: Minimum viable instrumentation on the four marketing pages plus the signup/check-email/welcome/onboarding screens (Vercel Web Analytics is a natural first choice given Vercel is already the host per `README.md`'s Stack section), plus UTM passthrough into the Checkout session's existing `metadata` object in `app/(auth)/welcome/actions.ts` (`metadata: { supabase_user_id, plan_tier }`, line ~165) so paid/referral attribution survives into Stripe.

### [HIGH] No contact or support path exists anywhere in the product — confirmed by the code's own comments, including at the funnel's one failure state
- Where: `lib/entitlements.ts:395-401` (`priority_support` feature comment), `app/(auth)/welcome/actions.ts:189-193` (checkout-failure error copy)
- Issue: This is not inferred from absence — the codebase states it directly, in two independent places. `lib/entitlements.ts`: *"comingSoon BECAUSE THERE IS NO CHANNEL AT ALL — not 'the queue is not prioritised yet'. There is no support address, no contact route and no help link anywhere in the product or in the marketing footer."* `app/(auth)/welcome/actions.ts`, on the checkout-creation failure path: *"No 'get in touch' clause: the product has no support address or contact route anywhere... so the old copy pointed a visitor at maximum intent toward a channel that does not exist."* The practical consequence: a visitor who reaches Stripe Checkout and hits a failure — declined card, Stripe misconfiguration, or (today) the confirmation-email blocker above — sees "Couldn't start checkout. Try again in a moment." with no alternate path, from a product that has confirmed to itself twice that none exists. This is the single highest-intent moment in the funnel (card already out) and it has zero fallback.
- Fix: Add a support/contact address — even a plain `mailto:` — to `app/(marketing)/site-footer.tsx`'s Account column and to the two failure-copy sites named above, before directing any volume of traffic at the funnel.

### [MEDIUM] No acquisition surface exists beyond the two indexable pages themselves — SEO, content, paid, social, and referral are all simultaneously absent, and the two live pages carry the entire load alone
- Where: `app/sitemap.ts:12-15` (exactly 2 URLs: `/` and `/pricing`), `app/robots.ts:39-77` (correctly disallows everything else); verified absent: no blog/resources route under `app/`, no JSON-LD/structured data in `app/(marketing)/page.tsx` or `app/(marketing)/pricing/page.tsx`, no social links in `lib/brand.ts` or `app/(marketing)/site-footer.tsx`, no referral/affiliate code path anywhere under `app/` or `lib/`
- Issue: The technical SEO plumbing that exists is correctly built — `robots.ts` and `sitemap.ts` agree, the noindex placeholders on `/terms` and `/privacy` are handled deliberately (see below), and preview deployments fail closed to noindex via `VERCEL_ENV`. But behind that correct plumbing there is nothing to crawl beyond two pages, and no channel modeled anywhere in the repo that would bring a stranger to either of them — no content/SEO surface, no ad pixel, no social presence, no referral mechanic. For a pre-launch product (`docs/MARKETING.md` claim rule 8: "there are no customers yet," and the custom domain itself has not cut over per `docs/LAUNCH-GATES.md` G5) this is largely appropriate to stage rather than a defect — the rubric's own early-stage carve-outs apply to Ads and Internationalization for exactly this reason. It is recorded here because once Finding 1 (email) and G5 (domain) close, there is currently no second lever anywhere in the repo to pull for traffic: the two live pages have to carry 100% of acquisition alone, indefinitely, until something is built.
- Fix: Out of scope for a strategy-execution fix — this is a build-out, not a bug. Smallest possible first move, if pursued: Organization/Product JSON-LD on the existing two pages (`app/(marketing)/layout.tsx` or per-page `metadata`) costs nothing structurally and is the only sub-finding here with a concrete, page-local home; everything else (content, paid, social, referral) is a channel decision outside this audit's remit.

### [LOW] README.md's lead pitch still carries the exact "workflow wedge" phrase `docs/MARKETING.md` documents as retired from every live surface
- Where: `README.md:3-6` vs. `docs/MARKETING.md:330-334`
- Issue: `README.md`'s opening description reads, verbatim and bolded: *"Log the trip once — your logbook draft and your invoice lines come from it, and your receipts attach to it."* `docs/MARKETING.md` records that this exact phrase (and its sibling, "Stop entering the same trip three times") was deliberately retired everywhere on 2026-08-17, and names the swept surfaces explicitly: "the H1, the tagline, the root SEO description, the OG card's baked-in text, the Solo tier blurb, the Overview and Trips empty states, and the help guide's summary." `README.md` is not on that list, and independent verification confirms the sweep otherwise held: the landing hero, pricing page, root metadata description, OG alt text, and the auth signup panel (which restates the landing H1 verbatim) are all consistent with the current money position, and a targeted search found no residual "trial" or duplicate-entry language on any live public surface. `README.md` is developer-facing, not part of `app/(marketing)` and never shipped to a visitor, so this is not a live public-copy defect — positioning coherence across the four in-scope public surfaces (landing, pricing, auth, metadata/OG) is otherwise clean. It's noted because it's the one place in the repo where the retired argument still reads as the current pitch rather than as a historical note, and because this document is what every other review agent in this audit is reading as ground truth for what the product claims about itself.
- Fix: Update `README.md:3-6`'s lead sentence to the money position, or mark it explicitly as a retired quote the way the file already does two lines below for the "expense file all post from it" correction (`README.md:8-13`).
